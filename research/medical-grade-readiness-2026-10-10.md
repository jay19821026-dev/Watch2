# Medical-Grade Readiness: Criteria and Project Gates

Updated: 2026-10-10

## Decision

The project cannot truthfully be called medical-grade today. FDA/TFDA/IEC/ISO citations, a downloaded public database, AI review, or passing software tests do not themselves calibrate this exact Watch 2 Pro hardware/firmware chain, validate a clinical claim, or authorize marketing. Code can implement traceability, quality controls, reproducible algorithms, tests, and submission evidence; it cannot substitute for the OEM signal contract, traceable reference measurements, intended-use clinical validation, a medical-device quality system, or market authorization.

Treat “medical-grade” as a product- and claim-specific regulatory outcome, not a score or generic certification. The app may still show consumer health measurements or explicitly labeled research traces, but none of those is thereby a medically validated result. Until the applicable authority pathway is confirmed and every applicable gate below has objective evidence, no disease, “normal/abnormal,” medical-unit, or clinical-accuracy claim is enabled.

## What authoritative sources establish

- FDA regulates software functions according to intended medical use and risk; a function that analyzes physiological signals for a medical purpose may itself be a device function. A disclaimer does not override the actual intended use, product behavior, labeling, or promotion. Start with FDA’s [Device Software Functions and Mobile Medical Applications policy](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/policy-device-software-functions-and-mobile-medical-applications) and [premarket documentation guidance for device software](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/content-premarket-submissions-device-software-functions).
- FDA’s [arrhythmia detector special-controls guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/guidance-documents-medical-devices-and-radiation-emitting-products/arrhythmia-detector-and-alarm-class-ii-special-controls-guidance-document-industry-and-fda-staff) calls for reproducible algorithm testing and reporting sensitivity/specificity; ECG rhythm detection is not made valid merely by plotting a plausible curve. The applicable submission route and acceptance criteria must be confirmed for this product and indication.
- FDA lists [IEC 60601-2-47](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfstandards/results.cfm?referencenumber=60601&sortcolumn=pa&start_search=1) for ambulatory ECG systems and IEC 60601-2-25 for electrocardiographs in its recognized-consensus-standards database. These standards must be obtained in the applicable edition and their applicability confirmed; a reference to a standard is not proof of compliance.
- FDA's recognized-standards database lists [ISO 80601-2-61, second edition (2017)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfstandards/results.cfm?referencenumber=80601&sortcolumn=std&start_search=1&title=) for pulse-oximeter equipment; ISO has since published [edition 3 (2026)](https://www.iso.org/standard/84595.html). Check FDA recognition/transition and applicability of the newer edition before using it in a submission. FDA's [pulse-oximeter guidance page](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/products-and-medical-procedures/pulse-oximeters) describes its January 2025 draft recommendations, including performance-testing concerns such as skin pigmentation. A raw PPG/SpO₂ HAL code is not an SpO₂ result.
- FDA’s [QMSR](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr) took effect February 2, 2026 and incorporates ISO 13485:2016 by reference for applicable manufacturers. FDA also maintains current [medical-device cybersecurity guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/cybersecurity-medical-devices-quality-system-considerations-and-content-premarket-submissions).
- For Taiwan, TFDA publishes medical-device software and AI/ML registration guidance; its [regulatory index](https://www.fda.gov.tw/TC/siteList.aspx?key=%E6%9F%A5%E9%A9%97%E7%99%BB%E8%A8%98&pn=1&sid=11652) lists current guidance, including 2025 updates. The function’s classification, evidence package, and registration path require product-specific TFDA determination.
- For EU market access, the European Commission’s [MDCG guidance index](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations/guidance-mdcg-endorsed-documents-and-other-guidance_en) lists MDCG 2019-11 rev.1 (software qualification/classification), MDCG 2020-1 (clinical evaluation/performance evaluation of medical-device software), and current cybersecurity and hardware-combination guidance.

These are regulatory sources and standards pointers, not a declaration that all referenced editions are the exact set applicable to this watch or its proposed claims. Confirm current editions, recognized scope, and jurisdiction with regulatory counsel/notified body/TFDA/FDA before freezing the development plan.

### Current-source update (checked 2026-10-10)

- FDA published a new final **Clinical Decision Support Software** guidance in January 2026 (re-issued January 29, 2026). Software that analyzes physiological waveforms/patterns or makes a patient-specific diagnosis is not made non-device CDS merely by adding citations or a disclaimer. Reassess each app function against the current [FDA CDS guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software) and [CDS FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/clinical-decision-support-software-frequently-asked-questions-faqs).
- FDA's [QMSR FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr/quality-management-system-regulation-frequently-asked-questions) confirms QMSR became effective February 2, 2026 and incorporates ISO 13485:2016 by reference. A hobby app repository, passing tests, or a written checklist is not a functioning manufacturer QMS.
- FDA's currently listed [medical-device cybersecurity guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/cybersecurity-medical-devices-quality-system-considerations-and-content-premarket) supersedes the September 2023 final guidance; use the current version for any applicable submission.
- FDA/Health Canada/MHRA's current [GMLP principles](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-devices/good-machine-learning-practice-medical-device-development-guiding-principles) inform lifecycle controls for AI/ML device development. They do not supply a validated algorithm or evidence for this watch.
- TFDA's official [medical-device regulation index](https://www.fda.gov.tw/TC/siteList.aspx?key=%E6%9F%A5%E9%A9%97%E7%99%BB%E8%A8%98&pn=1&sid=11652) lists the August 2025 revised CADe/CADx registration technical guidance and Taiwan's medical-device software classification materials. Classification and registration depend on each function's intended use; request a TFDA product-attribute determination where uncertain. The EU Commission's [MDCG 2019-11 rev.1 (June 2025)](https://health.ec.europa.eu/latest-updates/update-mdcg-2019-11-rev1-qualification-and-classification-software-regulation-eu-2017745-and-2025-06-17_en) likewise evaluates software in context of its intended purpose and claims.
- **Regulatory pathway wording is jurisdiction-specific.** FDA pathways include exempt functions/devices and multiple routes (for example 510(k), De Novo, PMA); determine classification and applicable controls first. Establishment registration/listing is not FDA clearance or approval. See [FDA How to Study and Market Your Device](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/how-study-and-market-your-device). In the EU, determine MDR/IVDR classification and conformity-assessment route; do not describe every route as government “authorization.” MDR Rule 11 classification may depend on whether software informs diagnostic/therapeutic decisions or monitors physiological processes; the exact claim/risk determines the class and assessment route. See [MDR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/oj) and [MDCG 2019-11 rev.1](https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/b45335c5-1679-4c71-a91c-fc7a4d37f12b_en?filename=md_mdcg_2019_11_guidance_qualification_classification_software_en.pdf). For Taiwan, use the [Medical Devices Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?media=print&pcode=L0030106) and TFDA product classification/registration rules for the exact intended purpose; where classification is uncertain, seek a [product-attribute determination](https://www.fda.gov.tw/tc/includes/GetFile.ashx?id=f637559984474447747&type=1). Taiwan QMS and license/registration requirements must be checked against the Act and applicable product category; see [TFDA QMS/QSD information](https://www.fda.gov.tw/TC/siteContent.aspx?sid=11584).
- **Do not assume a named standard applies by sensor name alone.** IEC 60601-2-47:2012 is scoped to continuous ambulatory ECG recording/analysis systems and explicitly excludes intermittent event recorders; its applicability to this watch's user-initiated capture must be assessed, not presumed. See the [IEC scope page](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/2666). The FDA recognized-standards database currently lists ISO 80601-2-61:2017 (second edition) as partially recognized, while ISO's newest edition is 2026; document both edition and FDA recognition status before selecting a submission standard. See [FDA recognized standards](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfStandards/results.cfm?ascapilotyn=off&category=&effectivedatefrom=&effectivedateto=&organization=&pagenum=10&productcode=&recognitionnumber=&referencenumber=80601&regulationnumber=&sortcolumn=pdd&start_search=31&supportingdocsyn=off&title=) and [ISO 80601-2-61:2026](https://www.iso.org/standard/84595.html).

### Independent project/code review

Two independent subagent reviews agreed that this repository and device evidence do **not** establish medical-grade readiness. One identified a calibration-terminology limitation: the earlier `EcgCoreResearchEngine.fitValidatedCalibration` accepted as few as five paired points in each train/holdout capture and checked user-supplied identifiers, timestamps, profile fingerprint, and numerical holdout RMSE. Production now calls `fitResearchCalibration`, the decision reason says `RESEARCH_HOLDOUT_GATE_PASSED`, and the previous API remains deprecated for compatibility. The numerical gate can reject malformed fits and support a **research-only paired holdout fit**; it cannot verify that the reference instrument is traceable, that the capture is authentic, that the data represent the intended device/population, or that the result is clinically validated. Five points remain only a minimal software guard, not an evidence sufficiency claim. Never treat a profile's `allowed` result as regulatory or clinical validation, and do not promote research mV to a medical result. The review did not examine private health exports or perform hardware/clinical testing.

The reviews also recommend explicitly distinguishing: (a) software/device classification from required premarket route, (b) U.S. clearance/approval from registration/listing, (c) EU CE conformity assessment from regulator authorization, and (d) latest ISO publication from FDA-recognized edition/extent. These distinctions are now reflected in this readiness plan; the responsible regulatory professional must still make the product-specific determination.

## What “achieve medical-grade” means for this project

There is no single generic “medical-grade” switch or score. For a supportable regulated claim, the legal manufacturer must select a **specific function, intended purpose, target population, output, user, and market**, establish the product classification/pathway, generate evidence for the exact hardware/firmware/software combination, operate the required quality system, and obtain the applicable authorization before making that claim. The current user request spans several independent modalities and clinical claims, so it cannot be achieved by one code patch or one public dataset.

### Work we can complete in this repository

1. Freeze and version software requirements, intended-use proposals, algorithm specifications, data lineage, risk controls, test plans/results, cybersecurity inventory, and change history.
2. Keep the release build in consumer/research mode by default. Medical claims remain disabled unless a claim-specific evidence package is complete and authorized by the responsible regulatory owner.
3. Enforce modality-specific units, provenance, quality, freshness, calibration and abstention gates. Keep raw source evidence immutable and prevent an unverified timestamp, channel map, unit conversion or inferred value from entering a medical output path.
4. Add deterministic replay, synthetic/reference fixtures, traceability from requirement to test, versioned algorithms, and reproducible analyses. These are engineering controls, not clinical validation.
5. Assemble submission-ready engineering evidence once the device signal contracts, reference equipment, frozen claim and acceptance criteria are supplied.

### Work that cannot be completed by code or AI consensus alone

- Assign legal manufacturer/product owner, regulatory/quality-system owner, and target market(s); determine exact intended claims and classifications with the relevant authority or qualified regulatory professional.
- Obtain the exact Xiaomi/LifeQ signal contract and permitted API details for Watch 2 Pro hardware and firmware (channel/lead mapping, timestamps/sample rate, units, gain/offset, filtering and known conditions). If unavailable, obtain traceable paired measurements on the complete watch path.
- Acquire applicable standards in their current edition and have applicability confirmed; FDA recognition of a standard does not establish that this product complied with it.
- Perform traceable bench and clinical validation with synchronized, calibrated reference equipment and appropriate ethics/consent, preregistered endpoints, representative population/device/firmware data, independent splits, prespecified acceptance limits, uncertainty/confidence intervals, and retained failures/unreadable cases.
- Establish and operate the applicable QMS, risk management, software lifecycle/change control, cybersecurity, usability, post-market and complaint processes; submit the appropriate FDA/TFDA/EU materials and obtain authorization.

### Immediate status / release gate

As of this review, **the medical-grade request is not achieved**. All existing sensor-specific gates below remain `NOT MET`. A source citation, AI-generated calibration, downloaded ECG dataset, high research-quality score, or consent checkbox cannot change that status. The app can continue to deliver consumer/reference and research outputs under their existing gates, but must not label those outputs medical-grade or turn them into disease diagnoses. The next decision needed from the product owner/regulatory lead is a jurisdiction and one intended first medical claim; the next device evidence needed is the OEM signal contract or a traceable synchronized reference capture for that exact modality.

## Implementation sequence

1. **Keep existing safeguards.** Preserve immutable raw evidence, timestamp/unit/calibration provenance, modality-specific quality states and explicit research labels. Do not loosen result gates to force a number or binary conclusion.
2. **Lock a single first claim.** The product owner and a qualified regulatory lead select one initial modality and exact intended use/jurisdiction. Do not attempt all sensors or disease claims in parallel.
3. **Obtain external prerequisites.** Request Xiaomi/LifeQ sensor API specifications and firmware scope; secure exact applicable standards; arrange a calibrated ECG/impedance/temperature/pulse-oximeter reference laboratory and a clinical partner/ethics approvals where required. Public databases can test algorithms but cannot establish the watch hardware’s calibration.
4. **Write design controls before collecting validation data.** Requirements, risk analysis, test protocol, endpoints/acceptance criteria, sample-size/statistical plan, data handling, version freeze, deviations and change control.
5. **Build a paired bench set, then a clinical set.** Capture synchronized watch and reference outputs under controlled conditions; keep subject/device/session splits independent; adjudicate labels; pre-register endpoints; retain all failures and unreadable segments.
6. **Implement only the narrow validated claim.** Bind each output to hardware/firmware/app/model versions; make out-of-scope conditions reject or show research-only; add traceable automated regression and release gates.
7. **Complete QMS, human-factors, cybersecurity, post-market and regulatory submissions.** Release only after the responsible authority grants the required market authorization and approved labeling exists.

## Evidence required to change a gate

Every `NOT MET` may change only after attaching a named, dated artifact with provenance, version/scope, reviewer, acceptance criteria, and result. AI summaries or agreement among agents are review inputs only. No gate can be marked met by filling missing values, changing labels, downloading an unrelated dataset, or using chip evaluation-board specifications as whole-watch calibration.

The immediate technical blocker is an OEM signal contract or a traceable, synchronized reference capture for one selected modality. The immediate organizational blocker is a designated legal manufacturer/regulatory owner and quality-system owner. Without both, the app can continue research engineering but cannot reach a supportable “medical-grade” claim.

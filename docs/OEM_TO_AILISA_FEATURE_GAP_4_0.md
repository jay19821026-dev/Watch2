# OEM → AILISA 4.0 feature gap / 原廠與 AILISA 功能差異

Checked 2026-11-08 against the installed Watch 2 Pro Xiaomi Health APK, privileged MiHealthService APK, supplied Mi Fitness APKs, this watch's `dumpsys sensorservice`, and AILISA source. This is a **source and runtime audit**, not a claim of OEM entitlement. Decompiled output is imperfect; identifiers and callback structures below are evidence of code paths, not permission to use private services or proof of clinical performance. OEM proprietary source, libraries and models are not included in AILISA.

2026-11-08 逐項對照原廠手錶 APK、特權 MiHealthService、使用者提供的 Mi Fitness 安裝檔、這支手錶的感測器清單與 AILISA 程式。表中的編號與回呼格式只證明程式路徑，不能代表原廠授權或臨床效能；反組譯結果仍需實機核對。AILISA 不包含原廠專有程式碼、函式庫或模型。

## Gap inventory / 差異清單

| Domain / 項目 | OEM call path / 原廠量測與調用 | AILISA 4.0 call path / 目前程式 | Gap and honest conclusion / 尚缺與可顯示結論 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heart rate / 心率 | Watch `health/HeartRateViewModel.java` uses Health Services; privileged `DataCollectionService.java` can separately collect optical HR sensor `65597` into green-channel raw CSV. Mi Fitness 3.53.0i/3.59.2i packs timestamped samples into Health Connect `HeartRateRecord`. | Wear Health Services → `WearDataProcessor` → `HeartRateWriter` → Health Connect; raw `MAX_HEART_RATE` `65597` can now be selected separately. | Standard BPM is shareable when it has a valid time, source and permission. Raw optical fields do not independently prove BPM. The current trend checks sample timestamps and source packages. |
| General PPG / 一般光學 | `DataCollectionService.java` labels `65597` heart-rate optical and `65596` oxygen optical. The live watch also advertises a distinct general PPG type `65572`; the inspected OEM collector does not use `65572` for those two CSV routes. | `RawVitalsProbe.Kind.PPG` reads `65572`; new distinct controls read `65597` and `65596`, all as raw research. | Do not merge channel identities. The witnessed general PPG run gave 8 events, 128 raw values, unknown sample rate and missing source timestamp on the renamed phone app; no pulse conclusion follows from it. |
| SpO₂ / 血氧 | Watch `health/spo2/Spo2ViewModel.java` starts typed OEM service `20485`; OEM raw collector uses `65596` red/IR channels. Measuring screen checks wear/contact. | Authorized Health Connect `OxygenSaturationRecord` reader and raw sensor pages; new direct `65596` raw capture. | OEM service percentage is not derivable from raw channel labels. AILISA may display a separately sourced Health Connect percentage with time/source, otherwise only raw optical evidence. |
| ECG / 心電 | Watch `we/b.java` takes sensor `65573`, skips 20 callbacks, requires field-10 marker `1000` and worn state, forwards ten leading values per event with 2 ms increments to `EcgJni`/`libfitness`; service-owned `ecgActivation` controls OEM UI. | Independent ECG `65573` capture and event-row research decoder; `HealthConclusionEngine` now shows fresh decoded event/point counts when available. | OEM activation, native result, lead map, mV scale and diagnostic validation remain absent. AILISA can conclude whether raw events and research waveform were obtained; it cannot label normal/abnormal rhythm from unverified timing/calibration. |
| BioZ/body composition / 生物阻抗與體成分 | Privileged collector writes ten fields from `65599` with load magnitude/phase, I/Q, raw/average I/Q and measurement/calibration-state headers. Watch `BodyCompositionViewModel.java` starts typed service `20487` and assembles subtype/value results. | `RawVitalsProbe.Kind.BIOZ` reads `65599`; research page stores event counts and channel values. User can open the OEM body-composition screen from the watch. | OEM typed body fat, water, protein, muscle and metabolic outputs are absent in AILISA. Raw BioZ cannot be converted into these without the device's electrode mode, scale and validated model. |
| Temperature / 溫度 | Watch `TemperatureViewModel.java` starts typed service `20500`, decodes a float, treats negative values as error and rounds nonnegative results for UI. Live watch exposes body `65590`, board `65589`, algorithm `65591`, alarm `65592`; OEM measuring screen also checks wear. | `AdvancedSensorController` and `RawVitalsProbe` collect body/algorithm raw channels, with separate time and wear evidence; OEM temperature entry is user-tapped. | AILISA has no verified equality between service `20500` result and HAL values, anatomical site or Celsius calibration. It reports a device raw value only. |
| Stress / 壓力 | Watch `health/stress/StressViewModel.java` starts typed service `20486` and parses its signed integer result; this is **stress**, not barometric or blood pressure. | Health Connect HRV and local research trends when valid inputs exist; watch has an OEM stress-page link. | OEM 20486 stress score/algorithm is not in AILISA. No local raw field is relabelled as that score. |
| Sleep / 睡眠 | Mi Fitness `SleepSegmentUtils.java` calls native `MiDevSleepAlgo` on device sleep bytes, then separates bed/wake, actual sleep, awake and optional stages. `SleepSyncBiz` maps recognized stage states to Health Connect types. | Health Connect session/stage reader, with source and partial-stage handling. | OEM native staging model and private source records are absent. A session span is not automatically actual sleep duration or REM/deep stage. |
| Activity/sport / 活動 | OEM watch sport screens and phone sport repository/Health Connect writers. | Wear Health Services passive/active metrics, workout route and phone Health Connect writer/reader. | Source, recording method, interval coverage and duplicate writer behavior need live reconciliation. OEM training-load/PAI formulas are not reproduced. |
| Guided breathing / 呼吸練習 | Exported `health/breath/activity/BreathMainActivity.java` has duration selection and heart-rate context. | User-tapped OEM activity link; Health Connect respiratory-rate records are treated separately. | A guided breathing exercise is not a respiratory-rate sensor. AILISA does not reproduce OEM exercise scoring. |
| Blood pressure/glucose / 血壓、血糖 | Inspected phone APK has **manual record** paths; the installed watch APK has no corresponding named health-measurement module in this audit. | External cuff entry and authorized Health Connect records; no watch optical glucose/BP claim. | No verified on-watch measurement path to enable from this evidence. |

The watch APK exports eight top-level OEM health Activities: heart rate, SpO₂, ECG, body composition, temperature, stress, sleep and breathing. AILISA links to all eight from a visible watch screen, but the OEM Activity decides whether its feature is available. Four typed OEM results (`20485`, `20486`, `20487`, `20500`) and the private ECG native result are **not** AILISA results. The corresponding raw channels and public Health Connect data are available independently when the device actually emits them and permissions allow access.

原廠手錶 APK 匯出八個健康主入口：心率、血氧、ECG、體成分、溫度、壓力、睡眠、呼吸。AILISA 的手錶頁可由使用者點擊這八個入口，但功能是否開通由原廠畫面決定。四種原廠服務結果（`20485`、`20486`、`20487`、`20500`）及私有 ECG native 結果**不是** AILISA 的結果。公開原始通道與 Health Connect 紀錄各自可用時，會以來源、時間、單位及品質分開顯示。

## Evidence and next verification / 實證與下一步

- 2026-11-08 on the renamed phone app, an owner-started general PPG run delivered **8 events / 128 raw values**, `sample_rate=0`, `timestamp_source=sensor_event_timestamp_missing`. This proves transport of raw values, not a heart-rate calculation. / 新版實機 PPG 已傳 8 個事件／128 個 raw 值，但時基未確認，不能推出脈率。
- The old phone package still holds prior research preferences (PPG 8 events, BioZ 432 events, ECG 221 events), while the renamed package initially had none. The existing migration bridge transfers the encrypted evidence database and profile, **not** the old diagnostic preferences. The two stores must not be treated as one current capture. / 舊版仍有歷史原始資料；目前搬遷橋接不包含舊診斷偏好，不可把舊資料當成新版即時量測。
- For each OEM raw channel, collect a worn-device event sequence, verify sensor type, field count, timebase and contact state, then compare a contemporaneous typed OEM or authorized Health Connect result. Only a demonstrated mapping and calibration can support a numeric derived outcome. / 各通道需取得佩戴狀態下的新事件，核對 type、欄位、時間與接觸，再與同時段原廠或授權 Health Connect 結果比對，才能驗證換算。

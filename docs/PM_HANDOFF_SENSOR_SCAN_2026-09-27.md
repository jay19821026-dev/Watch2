Then pre-register thresholds and report held-out sensitivity/specificity or error bounds. For SpO₂ specifically, additionally require OEM red/IR channel mapping and transfer/calibration plus an approved reference-comparison protocol. Without these, only deterministic transport/data-quality checks (finite count, sentinel ratio, timestamp monotonicity/gaps, array alignment) are in scope—not physiological estimates or disease claims.

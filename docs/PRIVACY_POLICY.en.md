# Xiaomi Health Bridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06 · Developer: Wang You-Jie (王宥傑)

Xiaomi Health Bridge is an open-source health-data research tool connecting an Android phone and a Wear OS watch. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency monitoring.

With explicit permission, the phone app may read or write Health Connect records including heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, steps, distance, calories, exercise/routes, weight, blood pressure, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, sleep, temperature, elevation/floors, and hydration. The watch app may use supported body sensors, activity, location, microphone, and Health Services. Data is used on the user's devices to display trends, provenance, quality and sync status, and to synchronize with Health Connect when authorized.

This open-source build has no developer-operated health-data server, advertising SDK, or third-party analytics configured. Phone/watch synchronization uses Google Play services Wearable Data Layer; Android manages Health Connect. Research captures and exports remain in app-private storage or a user-selected destination. User-initiated sharing is governed by the destination service.

Users can revoke Android, Health Connect, or watch permissions and delete associated data through Health Connect, Android app-data controls, or uninstalling the app. Debug logs and exports should be de-identified before sharing. The repository must not contain personal health exports, accounts, device identifiers, network addresses, or signing keys.

The app is not directed to children. For health concerns or emergencies, contact qualified medical professionals or local emergency services. Privacy questions: `s4140666@gmail.com`. Modified redistributions must provide their own policy and Data safety disclosures.

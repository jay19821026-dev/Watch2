## English

**Project title:** Xiaomi Health Bridge | Android × Wear OS Health Data Research

**Project URL:** https://ailisa.net/portfolio/xiaomi-health-bridge

**GitHub:** https://github.com/jay19821026-dev/Watch2

**Description:**

An open-source Android phone and Xiaomi Watch 2 Pro / Wear OS project by Wang You-Jie. It uses Wear OS Health Services, the Wearable Data Layer, and Health Connect to organize user-authorized heart-rate, activity, and other health records with visible provenance, timing, quality, and synchronization status. The 4.0 test build adds SpO₂ recording-method filters and explicit user-tapped links to OEM watch screens. Raw ECG, PPG, BioZ, and temperature channels remain research and quality evidence; without device-specific calibration and clinical validation, raw fields are not presented as medical measurements or diagnoses. The project publishes both source modules, test APKs, bilingual documentation, and dataset–paper–algorithm traceability. Not a medical device.

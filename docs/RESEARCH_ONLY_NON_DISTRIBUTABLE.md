# AILISA Research-Only Build

## Distribution status

This build is for private, non-commercial research and engineering validation only. It must not be published, redistributed, sold, uploaded to an app store, or provided to third parties without a separate legal and licensing review.

This notice does **not** grant permission to copy, embed, or redistribute copyrighted papers, paywalled datasets, proprietary SDKs, medical-device firmware, or vendor calibration coefficients.

## Permitted reference material

- Public-domain or explicitly open-licensed datasets and papers whose license permits the intended use.
- DOI, citation, abstract, formula summary, and locally written implementation notes.
- User-owned reference-instrument exports with consent and documented provenance.

## Restricted material

Do not package full copyrighted papers, subscription databases, downloaded publisher PDFs, proprietary clinical datasets, Xiaomi firmware, or OEM calibration tables into the APK. Keep only a source link, citation, license, checksum, and the minimum independently implemented method description required for reproducibility.

## Output scope

Sensor outputs remain research estimates unless device-specific calibration and the required analytical and clinical validation evidence exist. A private build label cannot convert an estimate into a medical measurement or diagnosis.
